My fitness routine cards

Morning Stretch

Perform 10 minutes of dynamic stretching to warm up your muscles.

Time: 10 minutes

Cardio Workout

Run on the treadmill for 30 minutes at a moderate pace.

Time: 30 minutes

Strength Training

Perform a full-body strength training routine using weights or resistance bands.

Time: 45 minutes

Yoga Time

End your day with a relaxing yoga session to improve flexibility and reduce stress.

Time: 30 minutes

Evening Walk

Take a leisurely walk in the evening to unwind and enjoy the fresh air.

Time: 20 minutes

Rest and Hydrate

Take time to rest and drink plenty of water to stay hydrated.

Time: 15 minutes
